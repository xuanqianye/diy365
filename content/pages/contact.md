## Copyright / DMCA notices

For copyright complaints and takedown requests, please do **not** use the general mailbox alone. Submit the [Copyright Removal Request](/dmca) form, or write to **diy365cc@gmail.com**. Include the URL of the material, a description of your original work and your contact details.

We aim to acknowledge valid notices within 24–48 hours and to remove or disable access to the reported material as required by applicable law.
