**Handmade** is a community website for people who love crochet, knitting and handmade crafts. Our goal is to make it easy to discover, share and archive free patterns, project notes and inspiration from makers around the world.

## What we do

- Give makers a place to publish their own patterns, photos and project notes.
- Help readers find free patterns and understand the stitches and abbreviations used in them.
- Credit original designers and encourage everyone to publish only content they created or are authorised to share.

## User-generated content

Most of the patterns on this site are uploaded by our members. We review submissions, but we cannot guarantee that every upload is free of errors or rights issues. If you believe any content infringes your copyright, please use our [Copyright Removal Request](/dmca) page and we will act promptly.
