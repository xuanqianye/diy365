## Copyright Policy

DIY365 respects intellectual property rights. We require that all submitted patterns be original works or shared with the rights holder's permission.

- We do not knowingly publish copyrighted patterns without authorization.
- If you believe your work appears on our site without permission, please use our [Copyright Removal Request](/copyright-removal/) form.
- Repeat infringers may be blocked from submitting content.

We respond to valid DMCA notices promptly and may remove alleged infringing material while a dispute is resolved.
