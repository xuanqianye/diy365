# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information **Handmade** ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to https://diy365.cc and all pages on this domain.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information**: username, optional email address, encrypted password and profile details you choose to provide.
- **Content you submit**: patterns, photos, titles, descriptions, comments and messages you send to us.
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, approximate device/browser type, referral source and interaction events, collected through server logs and third-party analytics tools.
- **Cookies and local storage**: used to keep you signed in, remember display preferences (theme, view mode) and to measure traffic.

## 2. How we use information

We use this information to operate the site, display and moderate content, prevent spam and abuse, improve performance, and understand which patterns are most useful. We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Third-party services

- **Analytics**: we use third-party analytics scripts (for example 51.la or Google Analytics) which may set cookies and collect anonymised usage data under their own privacy policies.
- **Advertising**: we may display advertisements served by Google AdSense or another ad network. These providers may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- **Hosting & CDN**: our hosting provider and content delivery network process technical data (IP address, request headers) in order to deliver the site securely.

## 4. Data retention and security

We keep account and content data while your account is active, and remove it on valid request unless we must retain it for legal reasons. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 6. Your choices

You may edit or delete your profile, request deletion of your account and content, or disable cookies in your browser. Some features may not work without cookies.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the bottom reflects the current version.
