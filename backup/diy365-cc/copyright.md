# Copyright Policy

**Handmade** respects the intellectual property rights of designers, photographers and publishers. All patterns, charts, photographs and text remain the property of their respective owners. Uploading content to this site does not transfer ownership to us.

## 1. What you may upload

Only upload material that you created yourself, that you are expressly authorised to share, or that is in the public domain / licensed for redistribution. Do not remove watermarks, credits or copyright notices. If you share someone else's work with permission, clearly name the designer and link to the original source.

## 2. Notification and takedown

If you are a copyright owner (or authorised to act for one) and believe content on this site infringes your work, submit a notice through our [Copyright Removal Request](/dmca) page or email **diy365cc@gmail.com**. A valid notice should contain:

1. Your name, organisation and contact details (email address, and where possible a phone number and postal address).
2. A description of the copyrighted work, with a link to the original where available.
3. The exact URL of the material you believe is infringing.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## 3. How we respond

We review valid notices and, where appropriate, remove or disable access to the reported material within **24–48 hours**, keep a record of the request and notify the uploader. We may request further information if a notice is incomplete.

## 4. Counter-notice

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, contact **diy365cc@gmail.com** with the URL, a description of the material and a good-faith statement explaining why it should be restored. We will review and respond in line with applicable law.

## 5. Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly upload infringing material will be restricted or terminated. We also take proportionate action against knowingly false notices.
