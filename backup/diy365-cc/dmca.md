# Copyright Removal Request (DMCA Takedown)

If you own the copyright to a pattern, chart, photograph or text published on this site and believe it was used without permission, please use the form below. We take copyright seriously: valid notices are reviewed and, where appropriate, the material is removed or disabled within **24–48 hours**, and a record of the request is kept.

## Before you submit

- Please read our [Copyright Policy](/copyright) for what a valid notice must contain.
- Provide the **exact URL** of the page you are reporting (for example `https://diy365.cc/post?id=123`).
- Only submit a notice if you are the rights holder or are authorised to act on their behalf. Knowingly false claims may carry legal consequences.
- For general questions that are not copyright related, use [Contact Us](/contact) instead.

## What happens next

1. We log your request and check it is complete.
2. Where appropriate we remove or disable access to the reported material and inform the uploader.
3. We update the status of your request. If we need more information we will write to the email address you provide.
